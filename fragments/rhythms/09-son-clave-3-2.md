The son clave, 3-2: x..x..x. ..x.x... over two bars of eighths. It is where the bossa nova clave and Funk's Bo Diddley beat come from, and sits halfway between Bossa nova and Funk.

Five strokes of a clave (`&claves`, General MIDI's 75), a sound of its own beside the drum bar's rim, at half the level (velocity 90), each stroke's own gain as Strudel writes it (`&claves:0:.5`, `s("claves:0:.5")`), so the drums performer plays the line as it stands (#287). `&claves` is a hole, a sound the core kit (or the General MIDI kit) fills with the line this fragment used to carry word for word (decision 054, rung 2: #244). Its other names (`&clave`, `&percussion`, its mix, with no card: where its notes go is the workbench's, #291) are none of the drum bars', so it layers on any of them in one text.
