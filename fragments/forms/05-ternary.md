The ternary song, A B A, as Twinkle has it: the call and its answer, the bridge twice, then the call and its answer again. Its hole is `&call`; `&answer` is its helper, the call two degrees down by default, and `&bridge` the answer a step up, as Twinkle's middle line is its second a step up. A piece names them to override them: `&song from ternary(answer=&wonder) &twinkle` is Twinkle whole, its bridge *up above the world so high*.
With a one-bar call it is 6 bars, with a two-bar call 12.
It taps the host's meter by the eighth (`step=8n`), and a splice plays its whole line at the splicing braid's rate, so a motif written in eighths keeps its rhythm in it. It has no voice and no card (#291): the lead performer plays it as its `&leadline` (`&leadline step=8n &song`), or a piece splices it where it likes.
At the tune's own point, not the centre of style space (#371): a children's song's form, as the facet `form`, at weight 0.5, so its distance counts double and it is found first only near the tune, not at a preset's point the centre's forms are nearer to (Techno, Ambient).

**Its signature** (decision 065): `&song from (&answer, &bridge) &call`. `&song from ternary &motif` takes a copy of it, `&call` its main argument; &answer, &bridge optional parameters, their lines their defaults.
