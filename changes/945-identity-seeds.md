### Added

- **A noise voice's grains are thrown from the cell's identity** (#932, #945, decision 098 amended): a voice of `s=white`, `pink` or `brown` that writes `seed=n` starts each note at a place in its noise thrown from `n` alone (`seed=n` is the place, not a salt beside it); one that writes none is thrown from its identity (what it plays and is heard as: no name, no label, no role, no part), so no edit elsewhere, no line moved and no rename moves its grains. Voices that are alike take, in turn, the identity hashed with their number, so none stack. **The grains of every unseeded noise voice move once** (the tutorial's `voice`, `drums` and `rhythm`, the library's fragments, the General MIDI kit); a voice with a written seed does not. A frontend that renders noise from its own lifted `noise.ts` keeps its grains until it takes the identity key (`grainKeyOf`). No event moves.
- **A fragment's hash is `e3:`** (#945, decision 106): the hash of its cells' identities, sorted, so a label, tags, a role, a part, a private cell's name or the order of its lines move nothing. A lockfile entry written `e1:` or `e2:` still checks as it was and stays as recorded (no warning, no rewrite); **a new lock is `e3:`**. `fragmentHashE2` and `identityOf` are new on `@yggi/cells/engine/use`.
- **`sameText` and `fileFragment` decide "same fragment" by that identity** (#945, #941): a text and its lines in another order, or with other labels, are one fragment; a collection's cells are paired by identity; a text of `from` lines alone is no longer the same as `stick_count`, `hat_count` and `clave_count` (Cell studio's Extract filed a vamp and a bass as one; its `fileOwn` guard can go once it pins this).

### Changed

- **A cell's identity leaves out what no listener hears** (#945, decisions 098, 106): `label=` `show=` `size=` `about=` `tags=` `part=` `role=` are no part of a cell's draw key, so an unseeded chancy cell throws the dice it threw under any of them (0 of the 96 unseeded examples move). `toCore` cuts `part=` and `role=` from a line that is no performer, and the measure of structure counts them free (BWV 847's measure is 1,113, was 1,114).
- **Normalise writes no seed** (#945): `SEED_ROUNDS` and the `seeds` rewrite are gone from `@yggi/cells/engine/normalise`, and the `kept.ts` seed exceptions with them. Seeds already written in the examples and fragments stay, as pins.
