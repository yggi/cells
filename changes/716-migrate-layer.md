### Added

- **`migrateLayer(layer)`, a learned layer read as the language writes it now** (#716): the counterpart of `migrate(text)` for the layer a frontend keeps and hands to each query. From any layer version it applies, in the order the forms were retired, `renamedLayer` (seed ids as words, v0.16.0), `signedLayer` (bass lines and progressions with their signature, v0.21.0), `axedLayer` (points on `sync harm bite mood`, v0.23.0, decision 076), `roledLayer` (each groove line's drum role written, v0.24.0) and `movedLayer` (the fragments v0.25.0 moved: Kinderlieder's tunes as its parts, `waltz` and `korobeiniki` renamed with their heads; `MOVED_IDS`, `movedText`). A layer at this version comes back as the same object. Each step is also exported from `@yggi/cells/repository`. A frontend that chained these itself (Jambot's `keptLayer`) replaces the chain, and keeps only what it retired of its own. Nothing a piece plays moves.
