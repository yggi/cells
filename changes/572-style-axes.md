### Changed

- **The style axes are `sync harm bite mood`** (decision 076, #572). `bite` (Bite: paddy to stabby) is articulation, as it was: how notes start and end. `mood` (Mood: darker to brighter) is what the mode says, moved by the tension in the harmony and the sound: `moodOf(mode, tension)`, from `BRIGHTNESS` (the scale's degrees, the third and the sixth counted twice) and a tension placed by hand or a quarter (`MOOD_TENSION`). Energy leaves the point (density is intensity, #571), and entropy stays the reach.
  - **The library moved by rule:** every association point's `bite` is its articulation. Its `mood` is the mood of the style it was placed for (within 0.15), else of the mode found at its point; a meter, rhythm, performer, module, form, expression or the band has none. Where two points of a facet differed only in energy (a second motif beside a first, the 6/8 and 3/4 beside Spooky's 5/4), the energy offset became a bite offset, so the second is still second.
  - **The styles** (sync harm bite mood), their moods placed by hand: Synth-pop .3 .45 .55 .5, Techno .12 0 1 .4, Deep house .35 .55 .65 .65, Ambient .05 .3 0 .9, Lo-fi hip hop .5 .75 .35 .5, Funk 1 .12 .85 .65, Bossa nova .75 .85 .45 .9, Swing jazz .35 1 .3 .85, Electro swing .55 .75 .6 .5, Spooky .2 .9 .3 .3, Kinderlied .1 .12 .5 .95.
  - **The default band reads `&style[bite]`:** the bass's cutoff and the keys' and the pad's envelopes, as they read articulation, and now the lead's cutoff, which read energy. Where a style's energy and articulation differed, the lead plays differently. *Radio*'s event snapshot moves (its notes stay); no other example's does.
  - **What a frontend must migrate at its next pin:**
    - `artic=` and `energy=` on a style, and a pick `&x[artic]` or `&x[energy]`, are errors naming Migrate.
    - **A style written in order, `map=style .3 .45 .55 .55`, still compiles, and now reads its last value as `mood`.** Its first three keep their meaning; its fourth, energy, is read as mood, silently. A frontend that writes styles in order writes them by name: Jambot's section texts do since yggi/jambot#618, and its `cellsAxis('artic')` / `cellsAxis('energy')` are yggi/jambot#597's. Cell studio reads no axes.
  - **Migrate rewrites an old text** (its rule `axes`): a style by name or in order, written by name on the new axes; `[artic]` and `[energy]` as `[bite]`. It is exact only against the old meaning, so Normalise never runs it, and a text written in order on the new axes must not be given to Migrate.
