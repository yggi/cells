### Changed

- **The style axes are `sync harm drive bright`** (decision 076, #572). `drive` (Drive: paddy to stabby) is articulation and energy as one, and `bright` (Brightness: darker to brighter) is the mode's, `BRIGHTNESS` in `engine/style.ts`: the scale's degrees summed with the third and the sixth counted twice, locrian 0 to lydian 1. Energy leaves the point (it is intensity, #571), and entropy stays the reach.
  - **The library moved by rule:** every association point's `drive` is the mean of its articulation and energy. `bright` is a mode fragment's own; a harmony's, bass's, motif's, stab's, instrument's or style's is the mode's found at its point; a meter, rhythm, performer, module, form, expression or the band has none. The styles: Synth-pop .3 .45 .55 .25, Techno .12 0 .9 .25, Deep house .35 .55 .65 .5, Ambient .05 .3 .1 .875, Lo-fi hip hop .5 .75 .425 .25, Funk 1 .12 .8 .5, Bossa nova .75 .85 .45 .875, Swing jazz .35 1 .45 .875, Electro swing .55 .75 .675 .375, Spooky .2 .9 .325 .375, Kinderlied .1 .12 .425 .875.
  - **The default band reads `&style[drive]`:** the bass's and the lead's cutoffs and the keys' and the pad's envelopes. The lead's cutoff read energy before, so where a style's articulation and energy differed, the band plays differently. *Radio*'s event snapshot moves (its notes stay), and no other example's does.
  - **On a style, `drive=` is the axis.** Elsewhere it is still a tape's or a ladder's saturation; written before `map=style`, it is an error that says so.
  - **What a frontend must migrate at its next pin:**
    - `artic=` and `energy=` on a style, and a pick `&x[artic]` or `&x[energy]`, are errors naming Migrate.
    - **A style written in order, `map=style .3 .45 .55 .55`, still compiles, but now reads `drive` and `bright` from its last two values.** A text written on the old axes in order plays differently and says nothing, so a frontend that writes styles in order rewrites what it writes: Jambot's section texts (`packages/score/src/section-text.ts`, `&style style=&(map=style …)`) and its `cellsAxis('artic')` / `cellsAxis('energy')` (`packages/engine/src/style.ts`) are yggi/jambot#596's and #597's. Cell studio reads no axes.
  - **Migrate rewrites an old text** (its rule `axes`): a style by name or in order, written by name on the new axes; `[artic]` and `[energy]` as `[drive]`. It is exact only against the old meaning, so Normalise never runs it, and a text written in order on the new axes must not be given to Migrate.
