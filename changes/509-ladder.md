### Added

- **The ladder: `ftype=ladder`, and `drive=` on a voice** (decision 074 §5, #509): a cell's low-pass as a ladder, four one-pole stages through a tanh with the last fed back, as superdough's sounds. Its core is the MIT one superdough adapts, lifted (TheBouteillacBear/webaudioworklet-wasm @ 0632e13); superdough's numbers around it are written from what they are, since superdough is AGPL and cells MIT: the input driven by e^drive (`drive=`, .69 unless given), its feedback min(8, 0.13 · `lpq=`), `lpq=` read as its resonance and not in dB, and its make-up gain. Past a resonance of about 31 it sings by itself. Its high- and band-pass stay biquads; on an instrument it is on its voices' sum, the instrument a stem of its own. `drive=` was a sound tape's saturation; it is a ladder's too, the same word for how hard a sound is pushed, each on its own scale. `ftype=ladder` is no longer a diagnostic. No example's text or events move. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** `ftype=ladder` asks for a ladder low-pass (Cell studio: superdough's `ladder-processor`, or cells' renderer), and `drive=` on a voice is its drive.
