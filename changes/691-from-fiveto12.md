### Changed

- A law is taken with `from` (decision 085, rung 5 of #691): `&cycle from five_to_twelve &day`, its clock the main argument, where it was `&cycle law=fiveto12 over=&day step=84bpm`. `fiveto12` is a built-in plugin-fragment, a fragment file like any (`fragments/laws/01-five-to-twelve.md`, `runtime: builtin`, kind `law`, no style: decision 090), resolved by the host's library; its id is `five_to_twelve`, since the example *5 to 12* is `fiveto12` and pieces and fragments share one namespace. Its block is the line the piece once wrote, so it plays the same. **Migration:** `law=` still reads, with a warning naming the `from` line, and Migrate rewrites it (the `law` rule); the examples *5 to 12* and *Daytime* are rewritten. `law=` leaves the reference, as an old spelling. Cell studio's `lawOf(c.params)` still finds the law: the expansion's head carries `law=fiveto12` and `over=`.
- `fiveto12`'s block uses the language's newest forms: its head is `&cycle [&notes * &level]`, notes with their levels (decision 087), and its place is its notes line's `pan=&space` (#739). `PluginFragment.voice` and `plays` are gone, `head` and `fragment` (its file's id) added (unreleased). It plays the same.
