### Changed

- A tap that names no meter is an error where a piece has several meters with a tempo of their own (decision 089, #757): it read the first of them in the text, so the order of two lines changed what played. Name the meter, `step=&waltz:4n`; Migrate writes the one it read (`main meter`), and it plays the same. A piece with one such meter, or a second meter that taps the first, is as it was. `CompileResult.meters` lists the meters where there is more than one; `mainMeter` is still the first. `daytime` writes `&tick step=&minute:4n`; no example changes how it sounds. Law L19 (the order of a piece's lines is irrelevant) checks every example in three other orders of its lines.
