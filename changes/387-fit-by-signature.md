### Added

- **The fit by signature: `useOn` and `fitsAs`** (decision 065, rung 4; #454, #387). `useOn(bench, id, { resolve, head?, main?, args? })` writes `&head from id(args) main` on a workbench and gives what the copy gives (`Used`). Without `head`, it takes the template's own head, numbered clear of the workbench's names (`&bassline2`). A template of several heads is taken by their names. Without `main`, it takes the workbench's cell that meets the main parameter: the copy compiles over it with nothing new said, a meter aside. Where several cells meet it, the template's own name for the parameter breaks the tie. `fitsAs(id, bench, options)` says whether the template fits there, and why not (`missing`, `sounds`, `unmet`, `diagnostics`, as `fitOf` does). A collection is judged as `fitOf` judges it. `holesOf` gives a template's `signature` (heads, optional, main). Two `walking_bass` uses over two harmonies fit and compile with 0 diagnostics, and every seed template fits a workbench that gives its main argument. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** the new exports. Jambot's `readLine` (`unionOf(walking_bass, bench)`) can move to `useOn`, and its mode repository can read `holesOf(text).signature.main` where it looks for `&tonic` in `uses` (yggi/jambot#562).
- **Alpha substitution, a core property, tested** (#387; [language/overview.md](../docs/language/overview.md#a-name-is-only-a-name)). Rename every name a text defines and it plays, says and is read as it was. `src/engine/alpha.test.ts` checks this over every example, with its `from` lines written in and as written. It lists, with why, each example where it doesn't hold yet: a drum's name on a MIDI card (decision 054), the names the default performer reads (the feel's parts and drum roles), and names bound by the library (a collection's cells, a performer's heads, the instruments' `&style`).
