It is Jambot's (`cadence` and `finale`, ending.ts): the dominant seventh on the key's fifth for the bar's first half, then the tonic, everything landing together on the half bar, the tonic ringing a bar from there. One head a part: `&cadence` (the chords), `&finaleBass`, `&finalePad`, `&finaleLead` and `&finaleDrums`; it takes `&key`. The dominant is written as the key's fifth and the tones above it by semitones (`[4,4####,4#######,4##########]`), so it is a dominant seventh over every mode, a leading tone in minor too, as Jambot's. Its clock is an argument, `&half` (the bar's halves by default): a score passes its ending section's (decision 016), and the ending starts with that section, `&cadence, &finaleBass … from finale(half=&endHalf) &key`, the section held two bars for the ring.
