### Changed

- **`toCore` writes gates and `pitch=` into the mix, and drops what nothing plays** (decision 069 §2; #482). A gate is its products: `&g play=&t^$-12 level=&acc*.4 [&a, &b]` read in a mix as `&g` is `(&t + -12) * &acc * .4 * &a, (&t + -12) * &acc * .4 * &b`, and the gate's line goes. A voice's `pitch=&x` is a sum in each product the voice is in, added to its notes: `[&t * &lead]` with `&lead … pitch=&vib` is `[(&t + &vib) * &lead]`. A note, a `label=` and the other attributes nothing plays (`show=`, `size=`, `about=`, `title=`, `tier=`) go. Left as written: a gate a role reads (a performer's part), a tape's read head, a gate over a kit, a voice read but in a mix or in a sum, and a pitch written in place (`pitch=&( … )`). A mix written over several lines is read whole. Over the examples, 45 of 83 are now wholly in the core (41 before), each rewrite S1-exact. Nothing a piece plays changes.
