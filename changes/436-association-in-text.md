### Changed

- **A fragment's association is written in its text** (decision 089, #436): on its declaration line, a template's signature (`&key from (&mode) &tonic style=(&deepHouse | &funk) mode=dorian`) or a collection's `* from` with no id (`* from style=(&synthPop | &deepHouse * .5)`). Every seed file moved; the frontmatter keeps `id` and its metadata. Each fragment is found exactly where it was (each derived association equals the frontmatter it replaces), and its `e2:` hash is unchanged, so no saved piece warns (`e2:` now also leaves out a lone point's weight, `style=&funk * .5`, which it kept before).
  - **`style=`:** a style of `styles` by name (a reference: moving a style moves the fragments placed at it) or an inline point naming any axes (`&(map=style sync=.75 harm=.35 bite=.65)`), `± r` a reach on an axis (kept, read by no query yet), `* w` a weight, `( a | b )` a set. **`facet=`** where it differs from the folder's or there is none. A template's **arguments** at its points are its optional parameters on a declaration line of their own (`mode=dorian`); `style` and `facet` are reserved. **No `style=`** is no style.
  - **The compile reads no declaration:** a signature may carry them, and its other attributes must be its optional parameters (an error names them); `* from` with no id compiles to nothing; `bodyOf` and a template's variants leave them out; signature lines that differ only in their declarations are one signature.
  - **A frontend reads `fragment.association` as before**, derived. `readFragment(markdown, { facet, styles })` and `writeFragment(file, options)` take the folder's facet and the styles a declaration names (`styleNamesIn`, `FOLDER_FACETS`); a file with a frontmatter `association:` still reads, for a release. `associationOf`, `readDeclaration` and `isDeclaration` are in `repository/declarations`. A fragment's text now has its declaration lines: a line that starts `* from`, or a signature, is no voice's.

### Added

- **A cell's `style=( a | b * w )`**, a set of styles found at each, as its association or wish (decision 089 §7); a pick of one (`&x[bite]`) and the section's `&style` set to one are errors.
- **A style point's reach**, `bite=.55 ± .1` in a `map=style` cell with nothing moving it: its value the centre, its reach kept (`CellDecl.reach`). With a moving cell (`± .1 * &walk`), it is decision 079's window as before.
