### Changed

- **`beat=` is retired: a meter counts its beats by its signature or its levels** (#502, #15). `meter("4/4") beat=2` is `meter("2/2", 2)`, alla breve, its bpm counting half notes; a waltz in one, `meter("3/4") beat=1`, is `meter("*..", 4n)`; on a scan, `beat=` gives way to `pulse=`. A text with `beat=` is an error; one read as old (`old: true`) still plays as it did, with a warning naming Migrate, whose new rule `beats` rewrites it, offered only where the rewrite plays the same notes. No example's or fragment's events move: `breve` is written `meter("2/2", 2)`. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** `beat=` drops out of the reference and of completion; a text a frontend stores with it fails to compile until Migrate rewrites it (Jambot writes none; Cell studio @ df4bb5b only names it in `docs/ui/midi.md` and `docs/examples.md`, whose lines want the new spelling); `migrations` can offer the rule `beats`.
