### Added

- **The braid's places read a cell as the piece is built** (#936, decision 107; amends 105): a count `x!&n` `x@&n` `x*&n` `x/&n`, `x?&p`, `x(&k, &n, &r)`, a pick `&x[&n]`, a turn, a move `&x^&n`, a rate's factor `step=&clock*&n`, `fifths(&n)`, `map=edo(&n)` and `map=ed(&n, &r)` take a cell that is one number, or a sum of numbers alone, as the number written, within the limits of the place (a count 1 to 4096; a refusal names them). One hole (the text range of the cell's name), one phase (`fillBuilt`: the line is read again with the values in, before the slots), no setter per place. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** nothing in a text that compiled before; no event snapshot moves. `BuiltHole` is now `{ from, to, ref, form }`, `BuiltLine` is new, and `CellDecl.holes` is `CellDecl.built` in `types/`. A `base=` line that reads a cell as built is refused ("write the number").
