### Changed

- **No name is the piece's by convention** (#387). The compiler reads no interface names. A `from` line binds what its signature declares, and a collection's lines read only what it defines, plus its sounds and its taps. The old `use` line keeps reading them, under `CompileOptions.old` (Migrate, Normalise). A score's harmony that nothing reads is still not called silent, but now it is told by what it is, a line of chords, not by its name `&harm`. A part of chords that nothing plays (a stab's) gets no info either. `canonicalText` and `sameText` keep the names a template's signature declares, plus the names a text uses and doesn't define, and number the rest: `&b meter("3/4")` is now `&bar meter("3/4")` renamed. The same goes for `identicalUpToRenaming`. `minify` renames `&bar`, `&key` and `&tempo` like any other name. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** nothing it plays changes, and no event snapshot moves. A filed fragment that differs from a held one only by an interface name's spelling is now that fragment renamed.
- **Deprecated: `INTERFACE_NAMES`, `unionOf` and `fitOf`** (#454): nothing in the package reads `INTERFACE_NAMES`, and `useOn` and `fitsAs` replace the other two. All three go in the next minor. The repository's queries still fit by `fitOf` until then.
