### Added

- **A song in sections** (`examples/song.md`, #810 rung 4): a verse–chorus song the default band plays, arranged by the song form Synth-pop finds (`verse_chorus`). Each section's intensity and players come from its kind; its tunes are sections of their own, a selector naming which each section sings.

### Changed

- **The radio plays in sections** (`examples/radio.md`, #810 rung 4): it takes the song form its style finds, `verse_chorus`, for its intensity and who plays, a section a step, and its `&song` walker turns once a song (64 bars, its set 256). Its sound changes on purpose: the lead rests in an intro and an outro, the stabs in a verse, and the band plays each section at its kind's intensity.
- **Electro swing writes its form once** (`examples/electroswing.md`, #810 rung 4): a selector over its four players (decision 061) in place of four level lines bar by bar. It plays the same notes and events.
