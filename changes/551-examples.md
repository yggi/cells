### Changed

- **`PRESETS` is `EXAMPLES`: the examples named as what they are, so a preset is only a style** (#551, part of #536). Every example piece, `examples/*.md` read at once, is now `EXAMPLES` (type `Example`, keys `ExampleKey`), from the root and from `@yggi/cells/engine/examples`. **Migration:** a find-and-replace of `PRESETS` → `EXAMPLES`, `Preset` → `Example`, `PresetKey` → `ExampleKey`, and the subpath `@yggi/cells/engine/presets` → `@yggi/cells/engine/examples`. For this minor version the old names stay as deprecated aliases, from the root and from `engine/presets`, so a frontend's next pin still installs and typechecks; they go in the minor after. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** deprecation hints where it imports the old names (Cell studio, about 60 imports from `@yggi/cells/engine/presets`); Jambot imports none. Nothing a piece plays changes.
