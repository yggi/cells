### Changed

- **`metric_accent` weighs a bar by its meter, not its count of steps** (#906, for yggi/jambot#749): its weights are the meter's grades, the family's meter contract's (the downbeat 1, a group's or the half bar's start .8, a beat between .6, an eighth .4, a sixteenth .2), from a new argument, `meter`: a signature or sizes (`3/4`, `7/8`, `2+2+3`), 4/4 by default, or the piece's own meter cell, `metric_accent(meter=&bar)`, handed as its levels. So 3/4 stresses its beats, not 6/8's half bar, and 5/4, 5/8, 7/8, 10/8 and every grouping of the contract get a weight on every step where they refused. `steps` is 0 by default, a sixteenth a step (16 in 4/4, 14 in 7/8); any other count is a whole number of steps a pulse, read on the same grades. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** 4/4 in sixteenths weighs as before, so the four seed grooves that take it play as they did and no event moves. `metricWeights(meter, steps)` takes a meter where it took a count (`metricWeights('4/4')` is the old `metricWeights(16)`), and `meterGrades` is new beside it; the fragment's text, and so its hash, changes, and a lockfile's recorded answer is answered again (its arguments now hold `meter`). A text that passed `steps=12` for four beats of three weighs it as 4/4 in triplets now, on the contract's grades.
