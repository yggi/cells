### Added

- **The melodic scales are fragments, found by style** (#719, twin of yggi/jambot#644): a new facet, `melodic`, in `fragments/melodic/`, the scale a lead walks over the key, each defining `&melo`. `plain` is the key's own seven (`&melo over=&key even(7, 7)`); `pentatonic` is five of them, a template whose phase says which (`&melo over=&key even(5, 7, &phase)`: 0 the major pentatonic's degrees, 0 1 2 4 5; 3 the minor's, 0 2 3 4 6), so it never leaves the key. The blues leaves the key (its flat fifth), so it is a necklace of its own on the key's tonic: `blues` (`&melo scale(blues, &tonic)`) and `major_blues` (`scale(scan(2 1 1 3 2 3), &tonic)`), which take `&tonic` where the other two take `&key`. Each is Jambot's `melodicHelix` for the same scale and side, note for note (`melodic.test.ts`). `plain` is found first, at distance 0, at every style of `styles` but two, so Jambot's ten presets each find the scale its lead plays there (`melodicDefault`, the plain one); Kinderlied finds the major pentatonic, Blues shuffle the blues, and the minor pentatonic is placed halfway between Swing jazz and Blues shuffle. `major_blues` has no style: it is taken by name. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** `FACETS` has an eighteenth, `melodic`, and `LIBRARY` five more fragments; no other query returns anything new, and no piece's notes or events move.
