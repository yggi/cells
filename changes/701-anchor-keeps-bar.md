### Changed

- An anchored recompile keeps the bar it is in ([#701](https://github.com/yggi/cells/issues/701)): a swing's share or a bend's depth moved (a slider rewriting the text) waits for the bar line, as decision 081 has a cell moving it do. The host's `load` with `anchor` answers from the piece loaded before up to the next bar line of its main meter, and from the new piece from there, anchored at that line; a window across the line is the two joined (its notes, its tapes' speeds, its MIDI clock's ticks). It waits whenever the new piece places the bar in progress otherwise than at another tempo; a tempo moved alone goes on as #56 has it, the beat in progress kept and what follows at the new rate at once, and a tempo moved with a swing waits with it. A `load` without `anchor` is as before. A frontend that swaps plans itself asks `keepBar(before, after, seconds)` (`@yggi/cells/engine/keepbeat`, also from the package's root): null where nothing waits (then `keepBeat` as before), else `{ line, at }`, the old plan's second at the bar line to play it up to and the new plan's second to go on from there (Jambot's seam). Cell studio's player, which loads with `anchor`, needs no change. No piece sounds otherwise.
