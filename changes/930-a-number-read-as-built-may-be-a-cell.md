### Added

- **A number read as the piece is built may be a cell** (#930, decision 105; amends 078, 093 and 101): `seed=&s`, `shift=&k`, `stride=&k`, `cycle=&n`, `laps=&n`, `chance=&p` and `size=&x` read a cell that is one number, or a sum of numbers alone (`&seed2 [&seed + 1]` is 8 with `&seed 7`), as the number written; before, each was an error ("read as the piece is built: a number, or free(…)"). One reader of a cell's built value (`builtNumber`, also read by a generator's arguments and `even`'s), one hole the scanner records at a place that fills cells (`NUMBER_PLACES`' `fills`), one phase that sets the attribute (`builtHoles`). So a template that draws at random takes a seed as an argument and can be taken twice with independent dice (`ticks(seed=&a)`, `ticks(seed=&b)`). **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** nothing in a text that compiled before; `chance=` and `cycle=` gain the limits their cells are checked by (0 to 1; the most steps a cell holds), which a slider's range was already held to. `BuiltHole` and `CellDecl.holes` are new in `types/`.
