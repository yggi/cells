### Added

- **A part read in its document** (#703, C5b): `readPart(document, head, { resolve, facet | part })` (`repository/readpart`) plays a part of a document as `readFragment` plays a fragment alone, with the document's key for it (`givenOf(text, head)`, `engine/use`: the document's own lines for the part's main parameter, which `partOf` leaves out). It returns a `PointReading` (the point, a confidence per axis, the intensity, the mode). A part open on its key reads the document's mode: Kinderlieder's `hinein` reads a dorian 0.60 over C and an aeolian 0.45 over D dorian. Read in a document whose key is the host's major it equals `readFragment`. The document's voice for a part moves no reading (a line's `bite` is its lengths'), and the meter and tempo are the host's 4/4. `readAudition` is the shared body of `readFragment` and `readPart`. Nothing is wired into a query or a placement yet; no piece sounds different.
