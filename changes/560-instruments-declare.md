### Changed

- **The instruments and the performers read only what they declare** (#560 rung 3c–e, its first of three; decision 075, the owner on #556 §3c–e). Decision 063 §3 is amended: the style is still the composer's, and a fragment that reads it declares it.
  - **The instruments** that read the section's style (`bass`, `keys`, `pad`, `lead`) are templates: `&bassV from (&style)`, its default the centre of style space, `&style map=style .5 .5 .5 .5`. Taken alone they play as they did with no style written. A piece passes its own: `&bassV from bass(style=&style)`.
  - **The performers** (`bass_performer`, `pad_performer`, `stab_performer`, `lead_performer`) declare their instrument's voice and the style as optional parameters: `&bass, &bassOut from (&bassLevel, &bassV, &style) &bassline`. Each default is the performer's own instrument at the centre. A piece that gave a performer its own voice (`&bassV s=…`) or its style by name passes it now: `bass_performer(bassV=&bassV, style=&style)`. Given no voice, the performer's voice is its copy's own, `&bass_bassV`.
  - **The band** takes each pitched instrument itself, `&bassV from bass(style=&style)`, and passes it (`bass_performer(…, bassV=&bassV)`), so a score's `* from band` plays and is named as before, its own `&bassV` still the one played. `bandAt` replaces the band's line for a voice as before. The band still reads the score's `&style` and its parts by name, until its own rung.
  - **Migrate's new `arguments` rule** writes those arguments. Read the old way (`CompileOptions.old`), a `from` line of one of these fragments that doesn't pass a parameter the piece defines a cell for passes it, with a warning naming Migrate (`BOUND_BY_NAME`, `engine/byname.ts`). So every piece plays what it played.
  - **The examples:** `electroswing`, `spooky` and `kinderlied` pass their voices (`bass_performer(bassLevel=&bassLevel, bassV=&bassV)`). Normalise then writes electroswing's and spooky's own voices as what they are word for word: `&keysV from brass_stab`, `&leadV from clarinet`, `&padV from organ`, `&leadV from theremin`. No `notes` or `events` hash moves; their `values` and `live` hashes do, and radio's, from the band's new lines.
  - **A copy's reach:** a parameter an argument binds to a cell no longer brings the lines only its default read (a performer given its own voice takes no copy of its instrument).
  - `alpha.test.ts`: `electroswing`, `spooky` and `kinderlied` hold as written for sound and diagnostics; only the feel (rung 2) keeps them listed.

  **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** a text that takes a performer with no voice and no style gets a performer whose voice is named `&bass_bassV` (was `&bassV`) in its cells and output ids, at the centre of style space. Jambot's section text takes the performers directly and writes `&style` and, at some presets, its own `&leadV …`: it passes them (`lead_performer(leadLevel=&leadLevel, leadV=&leadV, style=&style)`), as Migrate would write, or reads with `old`. A frontend that finds an instrument's voice by `startsWith('&bassV ')` reads `bodyOf(text)`: the signature line `&bassV from (&style)` starts so too (Jambot's `instrumentsAt`).
