### Added

- **A band-pass filter: `bpf=` and `bpq=`** (decision 074, #509): a voice's or an instrument's band-pass, its centre in Hz and its Q (linear, as Web Audio's band-pass reads it, the centre over the bandwidth, 1 unless given), each fixed or moving, after its low-pass and high-pass, as superdough has them; `bandf=`, `bp=` and `bandq=` are its other names. The renderer plays it as Web Audio's band-pass biquad. Two module fragments join `fragments/modules/`: `bandpass`, and `lowpass24`, the `lowpass` module's stage twice in series, superdough's `24db` written out. No example's text or events move. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** two new parameters to play (Cell studio: a `BiquadFilterNode` of type `bandpass` after its other filters, yggi/cell-studio#567), and two more modules in the seed library.
