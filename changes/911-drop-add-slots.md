### Changed

- **drop and add are slot operators on a line's steps** (#911, decision 052, point 11.3; a hard switch, no migration before 1.0.0): `drop &x i` leaves step i a rest, all it strikes; `add &x i` gives a rest what the line's loudest step strikes, at its level. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** a step struck by two drums together is dropped whole, never one of them; an add fills only a step the line leaves empty, with the loudest step's drum, not the first one struck. On E31's drum bars Vary gives 277 variations where Jambot's own gave 284 (the 7 a closed hat on a step its open hat strikes), each of the 277 one of Jambot's, 259 at the same index: a frontend that picks by a draw (`varied`) may pick another variation for the same draw on a bar that changed (Funk's). The `by:` strings are kept.
