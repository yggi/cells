### Changed

- A plugin-fragment's fills play in a piece (#792, rung 6a of #691). `from five_to_twelve` copies the fragment file's real cells into the piece: its head `&cycle [&cycle_notes * &cycle_level]`, its key, and each fill written `fill(five_to_twelve, notes, &day)` followed by its first lap's answer. Every reader sees those cells (the values and live views of *5 to 12* and *Daytime* move for that; their notes and events don't); realize plays a stream's laps one by one from the piece's own copy. `law=fiveto12`, the old spelling, is read as the `from` line Migrate writes, with its warning. `CompileResult.codes` holds each cell's line where a text has fills.
- **For a frontend:** no compiled cell has `law=` any more, so `lawOf(c.params)` finds nothing. **Migration:** `lawAt(result, clock)` gives the law that reads a clock (the sun's day), and `lawIn(result)` the law a piece plays (`engine/fills`). `filledAt` and `PluginFragment.block` and `head` are gone: the fragment file holds the block.
- **What a cell is** (#782): the kind `law` (a cell with `law=`) is `fill` now, a cell a plugin-fragment's code writes (`fill(…)`), since no cell carries `law=`. *5 to 12* and *Daytime* count five fills each, their head a phrase and their key a table.
