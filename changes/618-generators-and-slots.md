### Fixed

- **A key's mode cell and a style's slot are wires in the written graph** (#618, part of #825): `scale(&mode, &tonic)` and `scale(even(7, 12, &mode), &tonic)` draw the mode (and each cell `even` takes as k, n or phase) into the key, port `braid`, as the tonic is; and a style read by slot (`lpf=250..1600 * &style[bite]`) draws the style into the cell the compile makes for it (port `style`), which goes on into what reads it as before. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** 14 of 90 examples gain a wire and none loses one (15 added): ten keys gain their mode (`chords`, `swing`, `sympathy`, `worn`, `funk`, `twelve`, `sailor`, `patchbay`, `kinderlied`, and `daytime` and `fiveto12`'s `cycle_key`), and `style`, `radio` and `song` gain the style into their slot cells. `outlineOf`'s outputs tree follows the new wire: in a piece that reads a style by slot, the style nests under the first instrument that reads it (the default band's bass) instead of standing at the root. No event snapshot or `live` hash moves. `ResolvedCell` gains `valueReads` and `readsSlot`.
