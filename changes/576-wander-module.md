### Repository

- **`wander`, a slow bounded noise, and the Radio example on it** (#571 step 4, #576; `docs/notes/radio-walkers-measured.md`): a module fragment beside `lfo` and `sh`: six noises an octave apart (from its clock, a section of 8 bars by default, to 32 times that), each a value drawn afresh at its step and ramped to the next, summed with the slowest weighing most. Read by a jack or a style's axis (`bite=.55 ± .35 * &drift` with `&drift from wander`), it moves a little each bar and a lot over an hour: over 3 hours its largest change a bar is .012 of the axis at a window of ±.35 (the example's hand-written drift jumped by up to .54 a bar), and it remembers itself 136 sections (the example's, 1.4). It stays around where a hand or a host sets the centre, a bounded offset (choice A with C's tuning of the radio's note); a walk with no home needs state, and is #929's. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** a new seed fragment (the library holds 236); `radio`'s bite moves smoothly, so its events and values hashes change (`snapshot.txt`), its notes (276) do not.
