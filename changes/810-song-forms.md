### Added

- **The song forms, fragments of the facet `song`** (#810 rung 3): `verse_chorus`, `build_drop`, `groove_breakdown`, `head_solos_head`, `strophic`, `vamp` and `binary`. Each holds its sections in order, each a section kind taken by name: `&verseChorus`, the song's intensity a section a step, and `&verseChorusPlays`, who plays. A score splices them, `&intensity step=1m/8 &verseChorus` and `&form over=&band step=1m/8 &verseChorusPlays`; a song is its form's length, and comes round. They are placed by idiom, each style of `styles` in one, so each finds its form first at its own point (`npm run coverage -- --facet song`): Synth-pop, Chiptune and Electro swing a verse–chorus song; Techno, Acid, Happy hardcore and Elektro a build and a drop; Deep house, Ambient, Dark ambient, Trip hop, Lo-fi hip hop and Spooky a groove and its breakdown; Swing jazz, Bossa nova and a Blues shuffle head–solos–head; Kinderlied, a Sea shanty and a Waltz a strophic song; Funk and Salsa a vamp; Baroque and a March a binary form.
