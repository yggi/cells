### Added

- **A drum track is layers, and a fill, a count-in and the ending's drums are layers the form places by the bar** (#718, #720; twins of yggi/jambot#643 and #646). Each is a fragment a bar long, and a piece places it by a level line at the bar's rate, `[&fill * &fillAt]` with `&fillAt map=amp step=1m cycle=8 0!7 1`, as a groove places its euclid's hits by `&euclidLevel` one level down; no new form. The example `drumtrack` builds one: the sticks count in, the funk groove plays, a fill ends each phrase, the band lands.
  - **Fills, a facet `fill`** (`fragments/fills/`): E31's eight grooves are found as their fills too, a second declaration `facet=fill` at their own points and the styles whose idiom they fit, and a text takes the fill alone by its head (`&fill from funk_drums`); `abanico` (Salsa), `tom_run` (Spooky, 5/4), `waltz_fill` (3/4), `march_roll` (2/4) and `timpani_roll` (Baroque) for the rest. Jambot's own fill, `snare_run`, and its build's, `build_run`, are taken by name.
  - **Counts, a facet `count`** (`fragments/counts/`): `click`, a metronome from the meter, a click a beat at the meter's accent (`&accent [.9 + &bar * -.3]`: the bar .9, a group's or a half bar's start .6, a beat below it .3), so 7/8 clicks its three groups and 4/4 marks its half bar; `&accent` is a head of its own, a level line any line can be played at. `stick_count`, `hat_count` and `clave_count` are the click on a player's sound. Each takes the meter, `&count from click &bar`.
  - **The ending's drums**, `landing`: Jambot's `finale` bar, hit for hit, taken by name. The closing cadence and its ring are #720's next rung.

  Every style of `styles` finds a fill and a count at distance 0. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** `FACETS` gains `fill` and `count`; `LIBRARY` twelve more fragments; the eight E31 grooves' texts gain a declaration line, so their hashes change (`npm run fragments`' lockfile warns where one is pinned), their lines and events as they were. No piece's events move.
