### Changed

- **A draw is keyed on what its cell plays, not its name** (decision 069 §6, amended; #481). Chance (`x?`, `[a|b]`, `[a b|c]`, `chance=`) was thrown per seed, cell name, lap and step, so a text renamed drew differently. Now the cell is keyed on its flattened steps, its seed and its sound's parameters (`src/engine/drawkey.ts`): a text renamed, or respelled (a run counted, a phrase made a cell, a chance said on the cell or on the step), draws as it did, and two cells that play alike draw alike. A noise note's grain is keyed on its voice's place among the noise voices, not its name. **How pieces sound: every piece that draws draws differently, once.** The examples whose notes move: `chance`, `cellscape2`, `baustelle`, `focus`, `onemore`, `electroswing`, `brothers`, `ghosts`, `testtones`, `resonanz`; `patchbay` keeps its notes, its sample-and-hold's levels drawn anew. No Migrate rule is possible: a draw has no old spelling. Shorten now merges alike cells that draw, and `toCore`'s minify renames them.
