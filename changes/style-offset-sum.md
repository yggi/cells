### Added

- **An offset from a style, as a sum** (decision 100, #901): a declaration's point may be `[&acid + &(map=style bite=.1)]`, a style moved on the axes the offset names. Fourteen seed fragments placed beside a style are written so (the motifs `refrain` … `toll`, `wail`, `peek`, `haunted_progression`, `haunted_pad`), every point where it was. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** no query finds anything new; those fragments' hashes change, so a lockfile that recorded one warns once and plays the current text.
