### Changed

- **A style named in a declaration is read on its facet's axes** (decision 099, #900): in a meter, a rhythm, a performer, a module or a form, `style=&funk` is Funk's sync, harm and bite, without `mood`, as their inline points are. No seed fragment of those facets names a style, so no query finds anything new. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** a fragment of those facets written with a name is placed where its numbers would be.
