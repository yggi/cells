### Added

- **`ftype=`: the filters' model** (decision 074, #509): on a voice or an instrument, `12db` (one biquad a filter, the default, what `lpf=` has always meant), `24db` (two identical biquads in series, at the same cutoff and Q, superdough's 24 dB: what a filter then an instrument's filter at the same settings play, sample for sample) or `ladder` (a low-pass of its own, said and rendered as its 12 dB biquad until its rung). Superdough's numbers name them too (`ftype=0`, `2`, `1`): the compiler reads them as the words and Normalise writes the word. Anything else is an error. An instrument's `ftype=` is its own filters' and passes to its voices with its linear parameters. No example's text or events move. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** a new parameter whose value is a word; a voice's `params.ftype` is `24db` where it asks for two stages of each filter (Cell studio: two `BiquadFilterNode`s, yggi/cell-studio#567).
