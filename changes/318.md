### Fixed

- **A line of hits splices into another as its hits** (#318, decision 097's drum case): `&ref step=16n &hat`, `&hat[:4] ~ &hat[5:]` or `&hh &hat[1:]`, where `&hat` is a stepped line of hits, was the error "&ref has no voice"; it plays the hits the line written out plays now, on a card and through a gate. A drum motif may be stepped: a splice plays what the cell spliced is, in its step, its own `step=` aside, as a pitched line's splice always did. **And a spliced cell of rests is rests** (#342): `&fill step=16n &rest ~@13 &sd:0:.2 …` with `&rest ~@16` played its snares as picks of the kit through a gate (36, 37, 38) and was the same error on a card; it plays three snares at their levels now, as written out. **What a frontend sees at its next pin:** texts that splice lines of hits compile and play where they were errors, and a drummer's line that splices a bar of rest plays its own drums; no example's events move.
- **The alpha check reads what a name gave** (#944): where a line's name gave a role or a part, its value gives the same; where only its value gives one (a taken fragment's inner drum line, renamed `finaleDrums_kick`, or Electro swing drums' `clap`), that is more, not a difference, so an example may take a fragment whose inner drum lines carry roles. Electro swing, the one exception, is no longer one.
