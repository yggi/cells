### Added

- **`split` on a line of hits: a double** (#912, decision 052, point 11.4): a hit struck twice at its level in two halves of its step, on a line written step by step (`split &hat 0`: `&hh ~` to `[&hh &hh] ~`), never on a fixed step; the reader reads a step of hits in halves (`[&hh &hh]`, `[&hh:0:.3 ~]`), so a split groove is varied again. Asked by name: **what a frontend sees at its next pin:** nothing it doesn't ask for.
