### Changed

- **A backbeat is struck on the snare or a drum that stands for it** (#317): Vary's backbeat anchor read only the snare (General MIDI 38), so a clap on two and four was varied like any line. A line every note of which strikes the side stick (37), the snare (38), the hand clap (39) or the electric snare (40), on the meter's backbeat, is the anchor now (`BACKBEAT_DRUMS`, `vary/anchors.ts`). **What a frontend sees:** `anchorsOf` names the clap's line as `backbeat` and `variations` no longer offers to move it, in the three seeds that keep their backbeat on a clap: `electro_swing_drums` (14 → 9 variations on a 4/4 workbench), `acid_drums` (7 → 4) and `happy_hardcore_drums` (4 → 2); the other 30 rhythm seeds give what they gave, and over every seed on its workbenches the variations go from 818 to 808. E31's eight bars still give Jambot's 284 hit for hit, and no event snapshot moves.
