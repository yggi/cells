# 072 · `even` is the foundation: one maximally even necklace under the scales, the even rhythms and the meter

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted (the owner on #15, 2026-11-05: "yes, public even", "Table; euclid stays", "one shared even template", the graded key filed under #51) · Issues: #505 (this rung), #506, #507, #508, #15, #51 · Amends [027](027-on-top-of-strudel.md) (`even` was retired with it)

## Context

The meter review on #15 asked where a meter's default grouping comes from. Euclid's answer reproduced every signature the family has: ⌈n/3⌉ groups, turned to begin on a three. That answer is one phase of `even(k, n, phase)`, the function `diatonic` is built on (`theory/modes.ts`, `floor((j·n + phase) / k)`, its phase moving one place at a time). Measured on #15:

| set | in n | maximally even | generated by (a `fifths()`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| diatonic, pentatonic | 12 | ✓ | 5 |
| whole tone | 12 | ✓ | 2 |
| octatonic | 12 | ✓ | none (a repeated set) |
| the standard bell (12/8) | 12 | ✓, the diatonic's necklace (decision 056) | 5 |
| tresillo, cinquillo | 8 | ✓ | 3 |
| bossa clave | 16 | ✓ | 3 |
| 7/8's beats, 3+2+2 | 7 | ✓ | 2 |
| 11/8's 3+3+3+2, Kopanica's 2+2+3+2+2 | 11 | ✓ both | 3, 2 |
| 12/8's beats | 12 | ✓ | 3 |
| 10/8's 3+2+3+2 | 10 | ✓ | none (5/8 twice) |
| harmonic minor, son clave, rumba clave | | ✗ (one move from even) | none |

Where k and n are coprime, maximally even and generated are one (Clough & Douthett 1991; Carey & Clampitt): `fifths()` and `even(7, 12)` are two roads to one set.

**Chains of it are hierarchies, in both domains.** Each level is `even` inside the level above:
- **pitch:** ionian `even(7,12,5)` ⊃ triad `even(3,7)` ⊃ root and fifth `even(2,3,1)` ⊃ root; the pentatonic inside the diatonic is `even(5,7)`;
- **time:** a meter's pulses ⊃ beats `even(⌈n/3⌉, n)` ⊃ halves `even(2, k)` (the family's halving) ⊃ the bar.

Both chains grade in the meter's alphabet: 12/8 is `*..-..+..-..`, and C major's basic space (Lerdahl's root, fifth, triad, scale, chromatic) is `*,.,-.,+,.,.`. GTTM's metrical grid and its basic space are one construction, on the time wheel and the pitch wheel: #51's "one shape at every level", at its base.

`even(…)` was a form, and it was retired with decision 027: scales as `scan(…)` or `scale(name)`, rhythms as Strudel's `euclid`.

## Decision

1. **`even(k, n)` and `even(k, n, phase)` are a generator again, of a table** (#505). It gives k places in n, the same table as the `scan(…)` of its intervals, read as any table is: `over=` (a scale), a bar's steps, a pick.
   - **Its phase is its mode**, 0 to k − 1, each phase one place moved, every mode starting on 0. `even(7, 12, p)` is the diatonic mode of phase p, as `scale(&mode)` reads it. A phase of k or more would only lift the set, which is a tonic's job, so it is an error.
   - **`euclid(k, n, r)` stays Strudel's rhythm**, a braid of n steps (027). It strikes `even(k, n, r + offset(k, n))`'s places (037's offsets, `contracts/euclid`).
   - **Its place in the core** (decision 068): it is sugar, its `scan(…)` written, and `scan` is sugar too, its running totals written; the core (look up, dilate, shift with carry, combine) has neither. In the order of sugar, `scan` (any gaps: the written table, and as a clock steps of uneven length) is under `even` (the gaps as even as whole numbers allow: a dilate of k steps over n, quantised onto the grid of n, shifted by the phase), and `even` is under `euclid` (its places as hits on n steps, turned by 037's offset, a convention of Bjorklund's algorithm: hence `even` is the more fundamental), under the scale names of its phases (`scale(dorian)`, `scale(pentatonic)`) and under a meter's groups (#506). `scale(harmonic)` and `scale(blues)` name scans that aren't even. Note 418's rows 37a and 38 say so, and `core.test.ts` and `even.test.ts` witness them.
   - **A name stays the name.** `scale(dorian)` is `even(7, 12, 3)`, and both read; the named form is sugar over the general one, as `scale(name)` is over `scan(…)`.
2. **A meter is two applications of `even`** (#506): pulses into beats by `even(⌈n/3⌉, n)` for x/8 and x/16, turned to begin on a three; beats into halves by `even(2, k)`. The table of signature names goes.
3. **One `even` template under the fragments** (#507): `diatonic`, the single `meter` fragment and the even rhythms say where they come from, so the library states the identities.
4. **The graded key** (#508, under #51): the tonal hierarchy as a meter's levels. Its spelling is a decision of its own.

## Consequences

- #506 builds point 2, against `contracts/meter` v2 (yggi/music-commons#56): signatures by rule, a tier for each halving and for a group's pairs, grades mapped from the ends and the numbers a meter writes from the ends into five (the owner on #506), sizes with a unit and with 4 and 6, written levels' every mark a beat, the step as a note value. No example's events move.
- #507's first rung builds the single `meter` fragment of point 3: `&bar base=&grouping step=&tempo swing=&swing`, a copy of a meter being a meter (the owner on #507), its points carrying their grouping or swing; the thirteen meter files one. `diatonic` and the even rhythms written from one `even` template are its second rung.
- #505 builds point 1. `RETIRED_GENERATORS.even` and its message (#7's `euclidOfEven`) go, and no example's events move.
- Tests: `even.test.ts` (the seven modes and the pentatonics by S1, the tresillo and the bell as a bar), `parser.test.ts` (its places for every 1 ≤ k ≤ n ≤ 16 and 0 ≤ p < k; `euclid(k, n)` the same set turned), `contracts.test.ts` (`even(k, n, k − 1)` is `contracts/euclid`'s canonical set).
- What isn't even (harmonic minor, son and rumba clave) stays written. Whether "even, then one move" earns a name is open.
