# free()

A number left to the performer: a slider on the node, and moving it rewrites the `free(…)` in the code.

**`free(default, lo..hi)`** goes wherever a number is written ([decision 078](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/docs/decisions/078-every-number-may-be-free.md)): a rate, `step=free(90bpm, 60..240)` (the tempo); a shift, `shift=free(0, 0..6)` (a mode selector, which turning the wheel moves too); a gain in a mix, `&strings * free(.4, 0..1)` (a fader); an interval, `scan(free(2, 1..3) 1)` (the swing ratio of the Swing blues); a style's axis, `sync=free(.3, 0..1)` ([style.md](style.md); the example *Radio*); and a count, an index, a step.

- **A number read as the piece is built** (`cycle=`, `x!n`, `&x[i]`, `euclid(k, n)`) is the number its slider holds now. Moving the slider compiles the piece again.
- **A tempo moved keeps the beat** ([#56](https://github.com/yggi/cells/issues/56)): the piece compiled again goes on from where it was heard, its main meter's steps taken so far (the beat in progress, its fraction included) where they were, and only what follows at the new rate; the frontend passes the second it swaps at (the host's `load` with `anchor`, or `keepBeat`, `src/engine/keepbeat.ts`). A swing's share or a bend's depth moved so waits for the bar line, as a cell moving it does (decision 081, [#701](https://github.com/yggi/cells/issues/701)): the bar in progress plays as before, and the piece compiled again goes on from the next bar line of its main meter, anchored there (the host does it for an anchored `load`; a frontend swapping plans itself asks `keepBar`). A tempo moved with it waits too; a tempo moved alone doesn't.
- **A whole place** (a count, an index, a turn) takes a whole range, and its value is rounded; its slider moves by ones.
- **A range stays within the place's**: a count's at 1 or more, a probability's within 0 to 1.

**A cell is the playing's.** A cell (`&k`) or a range moved by cells (`lo..hi * &k`) moves only a number read while the piece plays: a sound's parameters, an envelope's slots, a gain. Where the number shapes the piece, a cell is an error that says so. Changing the program's shape while it plays is a selector's work.

A tempo marking is its range, centred: `step=free(adagio)` (largo larghetto adagio andante moderato allegro vivace presto).

A rate's unit can be an argument, its numbers then the core's (a second): `step=free(2, 1.5..2.5, unit=bpm)` is `step=free(120bpm, 90..150)`, the same slider shown in bpm. The unit written after the number, and a marking, are its sugar: `toCore` writes a slider so (decision 069). The unit is said once, after the number or as `unit=`, and only on a rate.

```cells
&bar meter("4/4") step=free(90bpm, 60..240) swing=&swing
&slow meter("4/4") step=free(adagio)
&swing swing=8n:free(2/3, 1/2..3/4)   # a share left free, its ends fractions
```

## A braid left free

**`free(0 2 4 ~ …)`** around a braid is the same thing for notes: steps left to the performer ([decision 018](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/docs/decisions/018-free-braid.md)). It is a cell's whole braid, nothing beside it.

```cells
&key scan(2 2 1 2 2 2 1)
&bar meter("4/4") step=&tempo
&tempo step=96bpm
&riff over=&key step=16n free(0 2 4 ~ 4 2 0 ~)
```

- **It plays as written** until a take is held in its place. Its **length** (8 steps here) is the frame a take fills, as `lo..hi` is a number's range.
- **A take.** Arm the cell (its sheet open, or *Play with* in the MIDI page: [../ui/midi.md](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/ui/midi.md#recording-a-braid-left-free)) and press **●** on the transport: play starts, and the next whole pass of the cell's loop is taken from the keys (a MIDI keyboard, the computer keys, the on-screen keys, the piano roll's rows). Steps are quantised to the cell's own clock, swing and moving clocks undone. Keys held together are a chord `[0,4]`, a key held across steps `4@2`, nothing `~`.
- **Held, not written.** The take plays in the braid's place until **Keep** (the cell's sheet, or *Save* on the transport) writes it, `free(0 4 7 5 ~ 4@2 [0,4])`; **Revert** goes back to what is written, and Stop lets go of it, as of every held value. Another take replaces it. The piano roll and the steps on its sheet edit a braid left free the same way: held until Keep.
- **Keys outside the table** follow one setting, for playing and recording alike: *allow* (as played; written with an accidental, `3#`), *snap* (the nearest entry) or *ignore* (nothing).
- Only numbers, rests and chords (held, repeated) go inside; not in a mix, a pick or `spell(…)` (where `free("…")` is a text).

The graph's sliders are described on [../ui/editor.md](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/ui/editor.md); a MIDI controller's knob can move them too ([../ui/midi.md](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/ui/midi.md#midi-learn)).
