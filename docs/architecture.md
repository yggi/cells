# Architecture

What lives where in the cells language, as Cell studio's architecture page described it when the language left it ([Cell studio's page](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/architecture.md) keeps the workbench's half: the layout, the audio, the UI). A name in `layout/`, `audio/`, `ui/` or `src/examples.ts` below is the workbench's.

## The flow

```
text
  parse()              engine/parser/     -> ParseResult (AST types: ast.ts)
  compile()            engine/compiler/   -> CompileResult (cells, clocks, outputs, tapes, diagnostics)
  plan(result, shifts, muted)  engine/realize.ts -> Plan
  windowAt(plan, from, length) engine/schedule.ts -> Window (plain data: what to play next)
  createHost()         engine/host.ts     the same as messages: load, window, grab, reset (a worker, this thread, node)
```

## src/engine


- `literate.ts`: literate cells ([decision 051](decisions/051-literate-cells.md), #195), the one reader of a file's code: the join of its ```` ```cells ```` blocks (`readBlocks`, `readLiterate`; a text with no fence is raw), the map from each code line to its line in the file (`fileLine`), the layout of what the code is not, and `rewrite`, which puts a changed code back into the blocks it came from. `piecefile.ts`, `repository/files.ts`, Normalise and `npm run structure` read through it.
- `piecefile.ts`: a piece as a file (`readPiece`, `writePiece`; [decision 047](decisions/047-fragments-are-files.md)), the same kind as a fragment's: YAML frontmatter (`title`, `tags`, `tier`, any other key kept; the subset `repository/yaml.ts` parses, a module with no imports), ```` ```cells ```` blocks among prose. The examples are such files.
- `parser/` (`parse`, `pieceMeta`: a piece's card, from a file's frontmatter and its text's first note, or from a text written the old way), `ast.ts` (`isNote`): one cell per line, read by construct:
  - `scanner.ts`: a line (continued lines joined), names, numbers, references with picks, shifts, `^n` and `^$n`.
  - `cell.ts`: a cell's line, its attributes and its braid in any order.
  - `braid.ts`: steps (numbers, rests, chords, subsequences, choices, cells) and their modifiers.
  - `generators.ts`, `free.ts`, `values.ts` (`step=`/`loop=`, `map=`), `units.ts` (a rate's units), `mix.ts` (a mix chord, kept as an expression).
- `attributes.ts`: every attribute in one table (what it means, takes and acts on; its doc page); the parser, compile, the node editor and the docs test read it.
- `algebra.ts`: the mix algebra ([decision 006](decisions/006-mix-algebra.md)): a mix's expressions typed by sort and distributed, one binding per voice.
- `program.ts`: a compiled program: its types (`ResolvedCell`, `Clock`, `Output`, `CompileResult` …), the `ROOT` grid, `MAX_EVENTS`, `BODIES`, `codeShifts`, and small readers of a cell (`pickIndices`, `pickSteps`, `adsrOf`, `isPositional`). `readers.ts`: the readers of a cell the engine, the layout and the components share (#317): `refsOf` (its channels, the `&ref`s in its braid), `ownRate`, `stepsPerSecond`, `lapOf`, `stepsAt`; the layout's beside them are `gridOf` / `heardPosition` (`layout/show.ts`), `envelopeOf` / `latestNoteAt` (`layout/envelope.ts`), and `midiOf` / `noteName` (`theory/modes.ts`).
- `helix.ts`: helix arithmetic, reading a program through the shifts: a cell's entries as turned (`entries`, `lookup`, `period`), a number resolved down to a grid (`resolveIn`, `resolvePitch`, `tableChain`), positions as pitches and colours (`semitones`, `positionHue`, `freqHue`, `gridName`).
- `compile.ts`: `compile()` and `compileSource()`; the phases are `compiler/`, run in order by `compiler/index.ts`. Each takes what it reads and returns what it makes (the cells pass along, each refining phase handing on a new map; the read-only phases get them read-only); they share only the diagnostics and the environment (`context.ts`):
  - `declare.ts` (declarations, the default grid, `scale()` tables, `over=` chains) → `builtin.ts` (no card written: the built-in card, an implicit `map=sample` declaration hearing every sound nothing reads; `CompileResult.builtinCard`: [decision 028](decisions/028-the-smallest-piece.md)) → `sorts.ts` (what each cell is, a mix typed: `algebra.ts`) → `resolve.ts` (cells as helices, `&refs`) → `bindings.ts` (kits, the sound card's channels, mixes expanded, voices) → `clocks.ts` (clocks, each cell's rate) → `checks.ts` (what physical and sounding cells refer to) → `outputs.ts` (one per voice binding, articulated; a voice alone, a drone: `Output.drone`) → `sections.ts` (each output switched by the selectors of the section that steps it: decisions 016 and 044) → `limits.ts` (the caps: notes a cell expands to, notes a second it starts) → `display.ts` (display tables, exciters) → `roles.ts` (attributes where they do nothing).
  - `tables.ts`: reading declarations as tables (`tableOf`, `lengthOf`, `unitsOf`).
- `realize.ts`: `plan` (every output's braid flattened once; drones apart, `Plan.drones`), `eventsBetween` (the notes that start in a window: tapes' echoes, laws, selectors, chance; a drone's held note where play began, `Held`); the gates the scheduler uses (`outputSounds`, `endOf`, `picksNow`, `liveGains`). Its parts:
  - `clocking.ts`: time. `timeMap` (swing, positions in a bar, a motor or a belt, a tap laid through its meter's map), `clocking` (a program's clocks at a moment), `shiftsAt` (tables turning), `playedCells`, `physicsOf`, `tapeMotion`.
  - `meter.ts`: the compiled `Meter` of `meter(…)` and `scan(…)`: its tree, its steps' positions, its pulse, beats and the note value of a pulse; `tapPositions`, where a tap (`pulse`, `beat`, `16n`, `1m`) steps in a bar ([decision 040](decisions/040-pulse-beat-and-laps.md)).
  - `flatten.ts`: a braid as numbers in its own steps (`flatten`, `unitsFor`, `stepsOf`, `StepEvent`).
  - `subseq.ts`: subsequences (`[0 2 4]`, `[0 2, 4]`, decision 027): where each step inside plays (`slots`, which flatten, the live view and the time view's structure share), what a step holds at the bottom (`leaves`), its numbers moved (`mapLeaves`).
  - `series.ts`: a cell read as a stream over a note (`modulation`, `levelSeries`, `paramTracks`, `pitchOf`).
  - `values.ts`: what a cell sounds at a moment, for views (`valueAt`, `valuesAt`, `notesAt`).
  - `ids.ts`: graph ids (`cellId`, `edgeId`, `stepEdgeId`), shared with `muted` sets.
- `flows.ts`: where sound goes, one description (#132): every flow between cells (a mix's entries, a sound card's channels, a selector's parts (the old form, removed: decision 016), a mix onto a tape, a tape under a head, `excite=`, `couple=`), with its graph edge id. Read by `routing.ts` (the graph's states) and by `audio/wiring.ts` (the connections `voices.sync` makes), so the two can't drift apart.
- `routing.ts`: what takes part in the sound, as graph nodes and edges: `routing` (what can sound), `liveRouting` (what does now), `drawn` (through petals), `nodeStateOf`/`edgeStateOf` (off, unrouted, on, idle, live). One model for nodes and edges: [ui/graph.md](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/ui/graph.md#states). `live.ts`: `liveAt`, the live view (playheads, lit table entries, and the live routing).
- `schedule.ts`: what to play next as plain data (`windowAt`, `tapeSpeeds`, `retime`, `isPhysical`). `host.ts`: the engine as a message handler (`createHost`: load, window, grab, reset; `planOf`, `grabIn`), what the worker runs; a piece's `use` lines read through the resolver the host is made with (`createHost(now, { resolve })`) and a `load`'s `locked`.
- `plugins.ts`, `builtinPlugins.ts`: plugins, the app's own and a piece's ([decision 011](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/decisions/011-plugins.md), [language/plugins.md](language/plugins.md)): `splitPlugins` cuts the `=== plugin name ===` blocks out of the text (blanked, so lines keep their numbers) and reads each manifest; the drawing stays code in `ShowPlugin.code`, run by `ui/plugin.ts` only. `builtinPlugins.ts` is the bundle the app ships (the sun: the same block syntax, as data, so compile knows its manifest everywhere; a piece can't take its names). `parse` carries a piece's, `compile` puts both in `CompileResult.plugins`, `layout/show.ts` (`pluginOf`) knows which cells they draw.
- `edit.ts`: line rewrites. `graph.ts`: `writtenGraph`, the graph of exactly what is written: each edge a wire (port, band, width, along the flow) carrying its transforms; the graph's types, its nets (`netsOf`). A cell is a meter in the graph (`isMeter`, drawn as ground with its net) when its bar keeps time, `meter(…)` or `scan(…)` alike: `keepsTime` (`program.ts`), a compiled `Meter` driving a clock of its own, not a tap of another's ([#123](https://github.com/yggi/cells/issues/123)). `transform.ts`: what a reader does to what it carries (a pick, a delay, degree, inverse, a transposition, a gain, a rate), as data, and the edge's label drawn from it (`edgeLabel`).
- `duration.ts`: piece length / loop cycle (`pieceTime`, `sections`, `mainTempo`, `anchoredAt`; `clockText`, the transport's time). `span.ts`: how much of a piece a render takes (`spanOf`, #315): the piece whole if it ends, one cycle of a loop, a minute of what never comes round, an hour at most, or what the caller gives; the one rule a MIDI file and any render read. `render.ts`: the renderer (decision 062): `createRenderer` takes a compiled piece's voices, `add`s events and `render`s frames, a stereo mix and a stem a voice or an entry of the card (`Output.parts[].entry`, recorded by `compiler/bindings.ts`), chunked or whole alike; `loudness`. `audioexport.ts`: `pieceAudio`, a piece rendered over its span (`pieceMidi`'s twin). `wavfile.ts`: `writeWav`, 16- or 24-bit PCM or 32-bit float. `noise.ts`: seeded noise for `s=white`, `pink`, `brown` (lifted from Cell studio). `held.ts`: `free()` values moved by hand, by their place in the code.
- `sameness.ts`: do two spellings play the same (`samePiece`, `sameNotes`, over `SAMENESS_SECONDS`): the promise every tool that rewrites a piece keeps (decision 049, proposed in #159). `rewrite.ts`: what those tools find, a `Rewrite` (its tool, its rule, what it does, `apply`), and `rewriteAll`, a tool's rewrites applied round after round while each plays the same.
- `identity.ts`: the text rungs of decision 050's ladder: `byteIdentical` (E0), `identicalUpToLayout` (E1), `identicalUpToRenaming` (E2, the learned layer's `canonicalText`), `normalFormIdentical` (E3); E4, E5 and minified identity are named there, built with the hash (#49) and the pure cell (#46). The sound rungs are `sameness.ts`'s: `soundsIdentical` (S1), `playsSameNotes` (S2), `sameNotes` (S3).
- `measure.ts`: a text's structure as decision 049 measures it (`structureOf`, `structureByCell`): each leaf a braid writes, one per attribute value, one per cell, over the normal form, names and comments free; Shorten ranks by it (#157), and `npm run structure` (`tools/structure.ts`) reports it.
- `normalise/`: Normalise (`normalise` in `index.ts`, decision 049): a valid text in its canonical form (which may change, add and remove cells, each rewrite S1: decision 049, amended), one rule a file (`names`: the interface names `&bar`, `&tempo`, `&key`, `&tonic` where unambiguous, and `scan(1 1 1 1)` as `meter("4/4")`; `aliases`: Strudel's or dough's name for a parameter as ours, `clip=` as `legato=`; `uses` and `forwarded`: a `use` in its one spelling, and a use of a fragment that only forwards as the use it forwards to); what it writes is [language/canonical.md](language/canonical.md), and `npm run normalise -- <files>` (`tools/normalise.ts`) rewrites files.
- `shorten/`: Shorten (`shorten`, `shortenAll` in `index.ts`), one rule a file (`runs`, `phrases`, `fractions`, `same`, `defaults`, `progressions`, `stereo`, `wrappers`, `aliases` (Normalise's rule, offered where ours is shorter); migrations: `gates`, `taps`, `swing`, `sections`, and `card`, whose rewrite is a piece file: `movedCard`), each returning rewrites, offered as suggestions measured by a shared `harness.ts`.
- `vary/`: Vary ([decision 052](decisions/052-variations-as-operators.md); `variations`, `varied`, `anchorsOf` in `index.ts`), the fifth tool: every variation of a fragment by one operator on one line, each a `Rewrite` of the tool `vary` that plays other notes (S2-distinct from its source and every one before it), its anchors and its holes kept, so `rewriteAll` never runs it; a draw picks one (`varied`), and which operators, which lines to `keep` and when to vary are the frontend's. `lines.ts` reads a fragment's lines (each entry of a card's mix: steps of hits, a subsequence, a Euclid, times a cell of levels) and writes one back as plain steps; `anchors.ts` derives the anchors (the kick's line, the backbeat's: `backbeatOf`, lifted from Jambot). Rung 1: rotate, drop and add; the pitched operators are rung 2.
- `time/`: the piece in time, heard from its notes ([notes/scalogram.md](notes/scalogram.md), [ui/time.md](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/blob/main/docs/ui/time.md)): `fft`, `wavelet` (the Morlet scalogram on a grid that thins for long pieces), `signals` (what a note is worth: impulses, envelope, voices, change, harmony), `boundaries` (cones, swells, novelty, scored against the sections written), `necklace` (foldings: a cyclic pattern at every level, the same one at several), `motifs` (a figure heard again, at another time scale), `spectrogram` (a waveform's peaks, a waterfall). For the time view, the probes and Shorten alike.
- `physics.ts` (motors, friction, belts: warps a clock's time map), `fm.ts` (Bessel spectrum), `wave.ts` (a voice's braid as waveform).
- `dough-params.ts`: sound parameter names. `order.ts`: the library's order of examples (`ORDER`: a new file in `examples/` needs an entry). `presets.ts`: `PRESETS`, every `examples/*.md` read at once (a piece file each: `source` its text, `file` the whole file), for the tests; `examples.test.ts` checks every text compiles clean, every file has a title and tags in its frontmatter and its card's `postgain=` (or no card: the built-in one), and that each reads the same as written the old way, the migration writing it byte for byte. The app doesn't use it: `src/examples.ts` has the build-time index (`virtual:examples-index`, made by `tools/examples-index.ts` through a plugin in `vite.config.ts`: title, description, tags, size, time, bytes, and the graph in miniature from `layout/miniature.ts`) and `loadExample(key)`, which fetches a piece's source (its own chunk) when it's opened; only the first piece is in the first bundle. `fiveto12.ts`: the day-long cycle. `ladder.ts`: the tutorial in tiers, `TIERS` (the forms each tier introduces) and `formsUsed` (which forms of the reference a piece uses, read from what it parses to); `ladder.test.ts` checks every tier's pieces against it ([display.md](language/display.md#the-tutorial-tier)). `tags.ts`: the kinds of tag (genre, artist, tutorial, topic) and the examples' vocabulary.
- Tape and resonator have no file of their own; they live in parser/compile/realize/clocking (tests: `tape.test.ts`, `resonator.test.ts`).

## src/repository

The repository of fragments ([decision 041](decisions/041-fragments-by-style.md)), in memory and stateless: what a jam learns is a layer the frontend hands each query ([decision 048](decisions/048-the-learned-layer.md)); of the language it reads only the parser, the compiler and the meter's taps, to read a fragment's holes and its fit ([decision 047](decisions/047-fragments-are-files.md)).

- `fragments.ts`: the format (`Fragment`: an id, cells text, an association of weighted style points, each with an optional facet, a hint), `fragmentProblems` (what is wrong with an entry), `createRepository` (throws on a malformed entry), its `query` (the k that rank first at a style point, over their points of the facet it names, by distance less the pull of their uses with what is chosen, a count that saturates, as strongly as the query's `pull`: `coUseScore`, [decision 046](decisions/046-facets-and-co-use.md); with a `layer`, over the seed and the layer merged), `merge`; the learned layer (`LearnedLayer`: filed fragments as files, points use added to the seed's, counts of use together; `EMPTY_LAYER`, `layerProblems`), `pointDistance` and `fragmentDistance`.
- `recognise.ts`: recognition ([decision 055](decisions/055-recognition.md)), `createRecogniser` and its `recognise(text, library, options)`: the fragments a heard text is within a tolerance of (the notes that differ, S2 as Vary reads them), each by its nearest path of Vary's operators (none, one, two at `depth: 2`) and the bar of a longer fragment's lap it was matched from, the library's readings kept by text so a recognition compiles only the heard text, and the heard text kept alike, so it isn't compiled again when it joins the next bar's library (#337), the 512 used last (`capacity`, #277).
- `learned.ts`: what changes a layer, each returning a new one ([decision 048](decisions/048-the-learned-layer.md)): `fileFragment` (a point at the query's point of weight `FILING_WEIGHT`, `source` and `created`; a text held already after renaming adds to its fragment), `useFragments` (each pair's count raised, and the point that found each fragment raised by `USE_STEP` to `USE_CAP`), `usedTogether`, `sameText` and `canonicalText` (identity until the hash: a text's own names numbered).
- `lockin.ts`: the lock-in measure behind decision 048's bound (#162): Jambot's radio walk at entropy 0.3 from each preset, in draws of 8 runs of 1,250 sections, the composer filing on a miss and using what it kept; `lockInDraws`, `lockIn` (what learning adds to the share among a run's 3 most used: per draw, pooled, and its standard error), `LOCK_IN_BOUND`. Its tests: the bound over 32 draws in `lockin.slow.test.ts`, a witness over 4 in `lockin.test.ts`.
- `holes.ts`: `holesOf`, a fragment's holes read off its declarations and its compile alone: the names it uses and doesn't define (`&harm`, `&key`; `key`, the main key, where a line reads it the old way, `over=key`), of those, the sounds (read only as a step or a mix's term, which an instrument fills) and the lines (read through an operator on a step, `&call^-2`: a form's hole, which a line fills, #314), the names it defines, and its clock needs (`ClockNeed`: a line tapping a meter it doesn't bring needs its lap to hold its braid a whole number of times, stated as the fewest taps a bar may hold, 16 for a two-bar clave of 32 sixteenths); `INTERFACE_NAMES` (`&bar`, `&tempo`, `&key`, `&tonic`, `&harm`).
- `fit.ts`: `unionOf` (the workbench, then the fragment's lines: a line given word for word kept once, a colliding private name renamed `&kick2`, an interface name never), `fitOf` (every use defined, no name defined twice, the clock needs met, the union compiled with no diagnostic the workbench alone lacks), `cachedFit` and `cachedCompile` (by text, what a repository keeps).
- `presets.ts`: Jambot's eight presets as style points, the points the meters', the rhythms', the bass lines' and the modes' files write as numbers; their tests read them from here.
- `yaml.ts`: YAML, the subset a fragment file's frontmatter is written in, parsed here (no dependency): block maps and lists, scalars (plain, quoted, decimal numbers, booleans, null; a timestamp stays a string, and the writer quotes it and YAML 1.1's booleans, `yes` `off` …, so js-yaml reads them as strings too), flow only as `[a, b]` and `{}`; it refuses the rest (anchors, tags, comments, block scalars, a second document, flow maps, …) with a message naming the line. `readYaml` (nodes with their lines), `writeYaml` (one canonical form).
- `files.ts`: a fragment as a file ([decision 047](decisions/047-fragments-are-files.md)): Markdown, frontmatter with the entry (`id`, `association`) and open-ended metadata kept in order (`source`, `tags`, `created`, `heard`, any key), ```` ```cells ```` blocks among prose. `readFragment` (the fragment, its `meta` and `prose`, or each problem with its line), `writeFragment` (a file written so reads and writes back byte for byte), `readFragments` (a library of files by path; an id given twice is a problem).
- `seed.ts`: `SEED`, the seed library read from `fragments/` through `seed.generated.ts` (`npm run fragments` writes it; a test fails when it is stale); `seedFragments(folder)`, one folder's fragments in file order. A seed file that doesn't read throws on import, naming its path and line.
- `meters.ts`: `METER_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/meters/`), their points associated as `meter`: thirteen meters, one line `&bar meter(…)` each, open on their tempo, associated by hand with Jambot's presets; four are 4/4s swung as Jambot's presets swing.
- `rhythms.ts`: `RHYTHM_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/rhythms/`), their points associated as `rhythm`, open on their meter (their lines tap it, no `&bar`): Jambot's E31 drum bars, one per preset at its point, and five claves placed by hand near Bossa nova and Funk (son 3-2 and 2-3, rumba 3-2, bossa nova over two bars; bossa nova in one bar too), struck on General MIDI's claves (`&claves`, 75).
- `bass.ts`: `BASS_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/bass/`), their points associated as `bass`: the five patterns Jambot's bass blends (`bass.ts` there: a root on one and three, the pump, the eighths, the funk line, the walk), each a line of indices into the chord in force (`over=&harm`, and `&harm[>>]` for the next chord), at the point where its share of that blend is greatest, with its card on channel 2.
- `modes.ts`: `MODE_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/modes/`), their points associated as `mode` ([decision 056](decisions/056-the-mode-facet.md)): one line each, `&key scale(mode, free(C))`, the main key the harmonies read, its tonic left to the host; aeolian, dorian and ionian at the points of the presets that play them, mixolydian halfway between Funk's and Bossa nova's; and the bell and scale, the major scale stepped as the West African bell, a `mode` and a `rhythm` at places of their own.
- `instruments.ts`: `INSTRUMENT_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/instruments/`), their points associated as `instrument` ([decision 054](decisions/054-instruments-and-use.md)): the General MIDI kit (`gm_kit`; the core kit, retired, forwards to 13 of its voices: `RETIRED` in `library.ts`), and one voice each for the bass (C2), the keys and the pad (C3) and the lead (C4), each holding its register (decision 058), with no sound of its own.
- `expression.ts`: `EXPRESSION_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/expression/`), what a voice does to its notes, associated as `expression` (#196): the vibrato (`&vibrato`, a voice's `pitch=`), a cell a piece takes by name.
- `performers.ts`: `PERFORMER_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/performers/`), their points associated as `performer` ([decision 058](decisions/058-the-performer-is-a-gate.md)): Jambot's drums, bass, pad, stab and lead, each a gate (`&bassOut` …) in its role cell ([decision 061](decisions/061-the-band.md): `&bass map=role &bassOut`) playing a hole the score fills (`&bassline` …, and its levels, `&bassLevel` …) on the instrument it uses, the bass an octave down (`play=&bassline^$-12`); the eight presets play them alike, so each is associated at every preset's point.
- `stab.ts`: `STAB_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/stab/`), the parts the stab performer plays (`&stabline`), their points associated as `stab`: the Charleston, lifted from the electroswing example.
- `motifs.ts`: `MOTIF_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/motifs/`), lines of one bar over the key, each headed by its own name (`&gypsy`), their points associated as `motif` (#294): eight by hand at Jambot's presets, and the electroswing example's call.
- `forms.ts`: `FORM_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/forms/`), braids of holes a composer fills with motifs, associated as `form` at the centre (#294): call and answer, the sentence, the period, AABA.
- `bands.ts`: `BAND_FRAGMENTS` (`fragments/bands/`), associated as `band` at the centre ([decision 063](decisions/063-the-score-and-the-band.md) §1, #391): the default band, `band`, which a score's `* from band` takes: the five performers under `&band`, the bass's and the lead's voices reading the score's `&style`, a sound card and a MIDI card on Jambot's channels.
- `band.ts`: which instrument a performer plays at a style, the band's choice read at resolution (lifted from Jambot's `instrumentsAt`): `instrumentsAt(point)`, `bandAt(point)` (the band with each found instrument's line for its voice), `scoreStyle(score)` (where a score's `&style` starts) and `resolveBand(score)`, a resolver that answers `band` at the score's style.
- `library.ts`: `LIBRARY`, the meters, the rhythms, the bass lines, the harmonies, the instruments, the modes, the performers, the stab lines, the motifs, the forms and the band, a hundred and nineteen.
