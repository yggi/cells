# Groove MIDI fixture

Three performances from the Groove MIDI Dataset, for `tools/groove.test.ts`: the files as they come, at their paths in the dataset, and their three rows of its `info.csv`.

- Source: [groove-v1.0.0-midionly.zip](https://storage.googleapis.com/magentadata/datasets/groove/groove-v1.0.0-midionly.zip), downloaded 2026-10-16; the dataset's page is <https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove>.
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as the dataset's `LICENSE` says. By Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas Eck and David Bamman (Magenta, Google), _Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations_, ICML 2019. The files are unchanged.
- The files: `drummer3/session1/8_jazz_105_beat_4-4.mid` (74 s), `drummer3/session2/16_rock_100_beat_4-4.mid` (62 s), and `drummer7/eval_session/1_funk-groove1_138_beat_4-4.mid` (28 s, too short for the sample: the test shows it left out).
